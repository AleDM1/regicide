Playing card artwork
====================

SVG playing cards by Adrian Kennard (RevK) — https://www.me.uk/cards/
Source: https://codeberg.org/RevK/SVG-playing-cards
Court cards are based on 19th Century Goodall & Son designs.

Released under the CC0 1.0 Public Domain licence:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

No attribution is required; this credit — and the link on the Ace of
Spades, which is kept intact — is given because the author asked nicely.

Deck variant: poker size (3mm corners), Fancy ace of spades, Diamond back
tinted #8b1a1a.

Local tweak: in 1B.svg / 2B.svg the diamond lattice pattern was scaled from
6 to 18 user units so the texture still reads at the ~38px thumbnail size
used for face-down opponent hands. The card faces are byte-for-byte as
generated by makeadeck.cgi.

File naming
-----------
  <rank><suit>.svg   rank = A 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 T J Q K
                     suit = S(pades) H(earts) C(lubs) D(iamonds)
  1J.svg / 2J.svg    jokers   (1J is used for the Regicide Jester)
  1B.svg / 2B.svg    card backs (1B is used for face-down hands)
